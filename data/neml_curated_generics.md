# Curated Generic Drug List — Source: Pakistan NEML 2025

Extracted from the National Essential Medicines List – 2025 (Ministry of National Health
Services, Regulations and Coordination, notified 20 October 2025). This is a deliberately
curated subset, not the full list — the full NEML contains 400+ entries including many
hospital-only, oncology, vaccine, and anaesthesia items that a pharmacist would never look
up by brand name at a community pharmacy counter. Selected for realistic community/
outpatient relevance and therapeutic category breadth.

All names below are **generic names as they appear in NEML** — the next step is resolving
each to real Pakistani DRAP-registered brand names.
